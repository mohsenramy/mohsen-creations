### 2. **Teamwork and Collaboration 

   *Common Questions*:
   - “Describe a time when you worked on a team. What role did you play?”
   - “How do you handle conflicts within a team?”

   *Preparation Tips*:
   - Use the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method to outline experiences where you successfully worked in a team or navigated challenges.

### 9. **Behavioral Questions: Handling Pressure and Mistakes**

   *Common Questions*:
   - “Tell me about a time you failed and how you handled it.”
   - “How do you manage stress during high-pressure situations?”

   *Preparation Tips*:
   - Highlight resilience and your ability to learn from mistakes.
   - Use specific instances to illustrate how you maintain composure and continue moving forward under pressure.

   *Example*:
   - *“Early in my career, I missed a deadline because I underestimated the time needed. I took full responsibility, discussed the delay with my manager, and implemented a more thorough time-estimating system that helped prevent similar issues.”*

### Additional Tips:
- **Use STAR Technique**: For each question, structure responses by outlining the Situation, Task, Action, and Result.
- **Ask Questions Back**: Prepare thoughtful questions to show your engagement and understanding of the role.
- **Self-Reflection**: Think about your experiences that align with the company’s values and mission to offer genuine, tailored responses. 

Practicing responses to these categories will prepare you for a wide range of HR and culture fit questions, ensuring that you can present yourself as both a skilled professional and a great fit for the company culture.
